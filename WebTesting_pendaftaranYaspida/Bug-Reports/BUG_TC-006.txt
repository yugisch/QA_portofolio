TC-006

Title:
Submitting the form and make one not proper input 

Steps:
1. Open the registration page
2. Fill the form with not proper input
4. Do the Captcha
5. Click Submit

Expected Result:
The filled input should stay the same and only give warning/error message to the not proper input

Actual Result:
it gives Error Message “Data sudah ada/Nomor salah/dummy. Silahkan cek kembali atau hubungi wali santri/siswa yang bersangkutan!”
But The filled form do not keep the input and back to blank

Severity:
High

Priority:
High
